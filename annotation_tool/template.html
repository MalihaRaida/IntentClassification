Annotation guidelines

Label what each comment does in context, not what its surface form suggests. Before labeling, read the diff, then the thread from top to bottom, then the target comment. Work independently: don't discuss items with the other annotator before recording your labels.

Steps

  1. Read the full context first: diff, previous comment, target comment, reply.
  2. Identify the communicative act. A question that works as a directive is RC, not RQ.
  3. Assign every label for which the comment clearly performs a distinct act.
  4. Rate your confidence.
  5. Write a one-sentence rationale. It is required when confidence is Low or you assign more than two labels.
  6. Flag any item that should be discussed at reconciliation, even if you labeled it.

Don'ts

  • Don't label by length: long comments are not automatically EX.
  • Don't infer unstated intent: no CN because you personally see a risk.
  • Don't conflate topic with intent: a security comment can be EX or RC.
  • Don't assign AK to every reply: a reply that only informs is EX.

Context in this tool

"Previous comment" is the review comment posted just before the target anywhere in the PR, so it may belong to another thread. "Reply" is the next comment in the target's own thread. Either may be missing.
